☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard </br>
📌 Project Overview </br>
This project presents an interactive Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product popularity, and store performance. The dashboard leverages Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, and Slicers to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

🎯 Objectives</br>
Analyze overall sales performance and key KPIs. </br>
Identify peak business hours and customer demand.</br>
Compare product category and top-selling product performance</br>
Evaluate revenue across store locations</br>
Analyze product size preferences and daily sales trends.</br>
Enable dynamic filtering using Month and Day slicers</br>

📊 Dashboard Features</br>
KPI Cards (Total Sales, Transactions, Average Bill, Average Orders)</br>
Hour-wise Quantity Analysis</br>
Category-wise Sales Distribution</br>
Top 5 Products by Sales</br>
Store-wise Revenue Analysis</br>
Product Size Distribution</br>
Daily Sales Trend</br>
Interactive Slicers</br>

🛠️ Tools Used</br>
Microsoft Excel</br>
Pivot Tables</br>
Pivot Charts</br>
Data Cleaning</br>
KPI Cards</br>
Slicers</br>
Dashboard Design</br>

📈 Key Insights</br>
Coffee and Tea account for the majority of revenue.</br>
Morning hours (8–10 AM) experience peak customer demand.</br>
Hell's Kitchen is the highest-performing store</br>
Espresso is the top-selling product.</br>
Customers spend an average of $4.69 per visit.</br>
Weekend sales are comparatively lower than weekdays.</br>

📌 Outcome</br>
The dashboard provides a clear view of business performance, helping stakeholders optimize staffing, inventory management, product strategy, and promotional campaigns through data-driven decision-making.
